TheClosedTest is a community-based closed testing platform built for Android developers who need help completing Google Play Console closed testing requirements.

Google Play requires apps to go through a closed testing phase with multiple testers before production release. Finding genuine testers can be difficult for solo developers and small teams. TheClosedTest helps developers connect and exchange testing support with other real users.

Play Store closed testing

Finding real testers for Android apps

Community-driven mutual testing

Improving app stability before production release

🔁 How It Works

Submit your app’s Play Store closed testing link

Accept testing requests from the community

Test each other’s apps during the closed testing phase

Collect feedback and improve app quality

TheClosedTest does not use automated testing, fake users, or artificial installs. Testing is done by real people within a developer community to support responsible app release and compliance with Google Play policies.
